# Stop messages

Copy and paste these. Don't soften them, because a polite maybe gets read as permission to continue.

**Duplicate job.** Send this to the agent that is behind, and keep the further-along copy.
> Stop this job. Do not continue. Another owner has it. Do not start related work unless I send a new brief.

**Correction.** Use this for a fix that takes under two minutes.
> Correction to [day]'s brief: [the one change]. Everything else stands. Do not ask questions.

**Too many questions.** Answer once, then send this.
> [Answer.] Everything you need is in the brief. Do not ask questions unless you are blocked.

**Standing lane hit its stop**
> This lane has hit its stop for the week. Do not run again until I send a new brief. Report what is unfinished.

**Kill a job after two failed corrections**
> Stop this job. Do not continue. I am closing it and will send a smaller brief.

**Out of lane**
> That is outside this task. Do not do it. If you think it needs doing, tell me and I will decide who owns it.

Don't rely on telling an agent to "forget." A reply saying it forgot isn't proof. Check the product's memory settings, and put the correct value in the next brief.
